# Polymarket trades and hourly bars

`src/download_poly_trades.py` downloads every trade of the 155 Polymarket
markets in `data/processed/pairs.parquet` and aggregates them into 1-hour
bars per market and outcome. All numbers on this page are from the run of
2026-11-04 (last trade in the data: 2026-11-04 23:44 UTC).

## How to run it

```bash
python src/download_poly_trades.py
```

The first run took 11 minutes (2,330 pages of up to 1,000 trades, at most 4
requests per second, no retries needed). Every page is cached in
`data/raw/polymarket/`, so a second run in the same session takes seconds.

## Files

| File | Contents | Committed? |
|---|---|---|
| `data/raw/poly_trades.parquet` | one row per trade: 2,246,500 trades, 18 MB | no (`data/raw/`) |
| `data/processed/poly_trade_bars.parquet` | one row per market, outcome and hour with at least one trade: 203,638 bars, 7 MB | yes |
| `data/processed/poly_trade_coverage.parquet` | one row per market: first and last trade, gaps, volume check (the table at the end of this page) | yes |

**Trade file columns:** `market_id`, `condition_id`, `token_id`, `outcome`
(`yes`/`no`, from the token id), `side` (`BUY`/`SELL`, what the taker did),
`price` (dollars per share), `size` (shares), `timestamp` (UTC, block time,
to the second).

**Bar columns** (`polymarket.trade_bars()`):

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `market_id`, `outcome` | the Polymarket market (Gamma id, as `poly_market_id` in `pairs.parquet`) and the token, `yes` or `no` |
| `bar_start`, `bar_end` | the bar holds trades with `bar_start` ≤ time < `bar_end`, UTC. Kalshi's candles are labelled by their **end**: join on `bar_end` = Kalshi's `period_end` |
| `last_price` | price of the last trade in the bar, dollars (0 to 1) |
| `vwap` | volume-weighted average price = `notional` / `volume` |
| `volume` | shares traded = sum of `size` (one share pays $1 if its outcome wins, like one Kalshi contract) |
| `notional` | dollars traded = sum of `price` × `size` |
| `n_trades` | number of trades |
| `last_trade_time` | time of the last trade: how old `last_price` is at `bar_end` |

Hours without a trade have **no row**. Nothing is filled in, so a missing
hour means "no trade", not "no data". YES and NO have separate bars.

## Trader identities are never stored

The Data API sends each trade with the trader's public profile. All of it is
dropped the moment a page arrives, **before** the page is written to the
cache (`polymarket.keep_trade_fields()`):
`proxy_wallet`, `name`, `pseudonym`, `bio`, `profile_image`,
`profile_image_optimized`, and `transaction_hash`, which leads straight to
the wallets on the public blockchain. The market labels (`title`, `slug`,
`event_slug`, `icon`) are dropped too. The code keeps a list of *allowed*
fields, so a profile field Polymarket adds later is dropped automatically.
None of the 2,330 cached pages contains any of these fields.

## What a "trade" is here

* **One row per fill, from the taker's side** (the API's default
  `taker_only=true`). The same fill never appears twice, so YES and NO
  volumes can be added up to get the market's volume. `side` is the taker's
  side. https://data-api.polymarket.com/v2/openapi.json (operation `get_trades`)
* **YES and NO are two views of one market.** The NO order book mirrors the
  YES book, so a NO trade at price *p* is close to a YES trade at 1 − *p*.
  The bars keep them apart; combine them yourself if you need one price
  series per market.
* **Fills under 0.01 shares are left out** (the API's default
  `filter_amount`). They are negligible.
* **Order within a second.** About 363,000 trades (16%) share their second
  with the trade before them. The API lists trades newest first by block time and
  then by a sequence number (no page was out of order). `trades_table()`
  keeps that order, so `last_price` really is the last trade of the bar.
* **Three-year window.** The market feed only goes back three years. The
  earliest paired market opened on 2024-08-02, so nothing is cut off.

## Coverage

### Is every trade there?

Polymarket's Gamma API reports a `volume` for every market (column `volume`
of `poly_markets.parquet`). It turns out to count **shares**, not dollars:
it matches the sum of our trade sizes almost exactly. The column
`volume_vs_gamma` of the coverage table is our volume ÷ Gamma's volume.

* **137 of the 150 markets** with a Gamma volume are within 0.5% of it
  (median 1.000). Five markets have no Gamma volume (1373479, 1373674,
  1373678, 654412, 654414).
* **5 open markets are 1% to 7% above** Gamma's figure. That's expected:
  `poly_markets.parquet` was downloaded at 19:56 UTC on 2026-11-04 and these
  markets kept trading afterwards.
* **5 CPI markets are 0.7% to 1.4% short**: 1559109, 1559117, 1559154
  (March 2026) and 1937952, 1937962 (April 2026).
* **3 April 2026 Fed markets are clearly short:** 669661 (81%), 669662
  (81%) and 669660 (95%). Every week of their life has trades, so this is
  not one missing stretch of time, and the cause is unknown. **Treat the
  volume of these three markets with care.** Their prices are probably
  fine: in the last 7 days before close, almost every hour has trades
  (164 to 169 of the 168 hours; an hour cut by the window's edge counts too).

Our dollar volume (`notional`) is only 7% to 83% of Gamma's figure (median
47%). That is a difference of units, not missing trades: a share traded at
$0.05 adds $0.05 to `notional` but 1 to the share count.

### First and last trade

* **First trade:** a median of 3.4 hours after Polymarket opened the market
  (at most 60 hours).
* **Last trade** of the 133 closed markets: a median of 0.7 hours before
  Polymarket closed the market (at most 20 hours). In 10 markets the last
  trade is up to 12 minutes *after* Gamma's `closed_time`: block time and
  Gamma's close stamp are not exactly the same clock.
* 22 markets are still open (20 Fed, 2 CPI; meetings and releases from
  October 2026 to April 2027). Their data stops at the download time.

### Gaps

A gap is the time between two consecutive trades of a market (YES and NO
together).

| | Fed (76 markets) | CPI (79 markets) |
|---|---:|---:|
| trades | 2,163,878 | 82,622 |
| trades per market, median | 22,561 | 792 |
| days from first to last trade, median | 132 | 30 |
| hours with a trade (first to last trade), median | 57% | 30% |
| hours with a trade in the last 7 days before close, median (closed markets) | 100% | 51% |
| longest gap, median | 39 h | 34 h |
| longest gap, maximum | 445 h (906976) | 226 h (3539660) |
| gaps over 24 h per market, median | 2 | 2 |

* **The long gaps are early in a market's life.** In 132 of the 155 markets
  the longest gap falls in the first half of the time from first to last
  trade, typically in the quiet weeks after a market opens. The longest gaps
  of all are in the June 2026 Fed markets, opened in December 2025: right
  after opening they had no trade at all for 8 to 19 days.
* **Near the event, Fed markets trade every hour.** In the last 7 days
  before close almost every hour has a trade (median 100%, lowest 42%). CPI
  markets are thinner: a median of 51% of the hours in their last week
  (lowest 2%). For CPI pairs, hourly comparisons with Kalshi will often have
  no Polymarket trade in the hour. Use `last_trade_time` to see how stale a
  price is, or fall back to the order-book price series in
  `poly_prices.parquet`.
* 35 markets have no gap longer than 24 hours.
* The thinnest markets are extreme buckets: 5691235 (CPI "0.9% or more" in
  October 2026, 30 trades) and the April 2027 Fed "50+ bps" buckets, which
  opened on 2026-10-28 (44 and 46 trades).
